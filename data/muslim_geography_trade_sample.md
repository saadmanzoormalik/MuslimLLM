Muslim merchants, scholars, sailors, and travelers connected regions across the Indian Ocean, Mediterranean, Sahara, Silk Roads, and East African coast. Trade networks moved goods, legal norms, languages, technologies, and scholarly ideas.

Analysis should distinguish between commercial exchange, state power, religious transmission, and local adaptation. Geography matters: Andalusia, Mali, Gujarat, Aceh, Cairo, Istanbul, and Bukhara participated in different ways.
