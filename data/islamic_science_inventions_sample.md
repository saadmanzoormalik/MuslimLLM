Scholars working in Muslim-ruled societies contributed to mathematics, astronomy, medicine, optics, geography, engineering, pharmacology, and translation movements. Figures often discussed include al-Khwarizmi in algebra, Ibn al-Haytham in optics, al-Biruni in geography and astronomy, and physicians associated with hospitals and medical teaching.

Historical claims should be specific, avoid civilizational boasting without evidence, and separate original contribution, preservation, translation, synthesis, and transmission.
