Zakat is an obligatory act of worship connected to wealth. It is due on eligible forms of wealth when the required threshold, known as nisab, and other conditions are met.

Zakat is not merely general charity. It is a structured obligation that purifies wealth, supports social welfare, and directs resources to eligible recipient categories recognized in Islamic law.

Common discussions around zakat include nisab, the passing of a lunar year for many types of wealth, eligible assets, eligible recipients, and the difference between obligatory zakat and voluntary sadaqah.

Exact calculations depend on the asset type and legal details. A personal ruling or exact calculation should be checked with a qualified scholar or reliable zakat authority.
